# Privacy notes

The public dataset intentionally removes Telasi subscriber/account numbers.

Do not commit:
- the mapping between `SITE_A` / `SITE_B` and real subscriber numbers;
- exact residential addresses unless publication is necessary and consented to;
- phone screenshots containing unrelated personal data.

For a formal complaint to Telasi, the complainant can provide the relevant subscriber number privately in the complaint itself while citing this public dataset for the longitudinal evidence.
